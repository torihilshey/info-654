About Oma's Haus

Since 1972, Oma's Haus has been New York City's premier destination to experience authentic German cuisine, live music and festivals. The bar was opened by a German Shepherd dog that ironically has an Irish name, Molly (she's pretty talented!). This tudor style historic property offers a unique blend of traditional German hospitality and modern amenities. Oma's Haus is a lovely and versatile space, intentionally designed to bring together the finest elements of German culture. We hope that every trip to Oma's Haus is a memorable one.


One of the premiere spaces on the property is the listening lounge. In 2026 the owners began renovations to enhance the space, and guests can now enjoy music from Molly's extensive vinyl record collection heard on a Dolby Atmos surround sound system. The listening lounge features a working fireplace and is the perfect spot to come together with family and friends to share a love for music. Coming soon this winter, the listening lounge will feature expanded seating areas and be ready to host themed listening nights, and karaoke.


Another popular area at Oma's Haus is the outdoor biergarten, where guests can enjoy a relaxed and peaceful open-air atmosphere while sipping on German imported beers from some of the world's oldest breweries. The concept and term biergarten originated in Bavaria during the 19th century and is a German term for beer garden. The biergarten at Oma's Haus offers a cozy and comfortable environment while you soak in the charm of the outdoors. It's the perfect place to catch your breath and unwind with family and friends.
